          Incident Playbook

          Steps When an Alert Fires

          06:00 – 21:00 IST
          1. 1
            0 to 5 mins: Acknowledge

            Acknowledge the alert in SolarWinds and post "on it" in the incident channel.

          2. 2
            Within 15 mins: Restore Service First

            Start active work within 15 minutes. Restore service first (restart, failover, rollback); investigate root cause later.

          3. 3
            At 15 mins: Escalate if Stuck or Customer Down

            Stuck after 15 minutes or customer service is down? Call for help and tell Vishnu immediately.

          4. 4
            Every 30 mins: Status Updates

            Post a progress update in the incident channel every 30 minutes (and update the customer if service is down).

          5. 5
            Resolution & Self-Recovered Alerts

            Resolve in SolarWinds with a one-line note. Even if it recovered on its own: confirm health, find the cause, and record it.

          Cautions & Non-Negotiables

          Golden Rule

          "Can't respond for any reason? Say so in the channel and call the secondary or Vishnu right away. Silence is the only wrong answer."

          Missed Pages Policy

          • First miss: One-to-one with Vishnu to fix the cause.
          • Second miss (within 3 months): Written warning.

          The Basics & Reachability Notes

          Who is on call? Whoever SolarWinds shows. A verbal or WhatsApp swap does not count.
          Owner of the incident The weekly on-call engineer. They can summon anyone to help and stay the lead.
          Reachability & Equipment You don't need to be at your desk, but you must be reachable: Phone ON and LOUD (not silent or DND), laptop and hotspot with you, able to start work within 15 minutes.

          Leave & Shift Coverage Rules

          Planned Leave or Travel (3 Days Ahead)
          1. Find a colleague to cover your shift.
          2. Set the override in SolarWinds.
          3. Email the team, copying Vishnu: dates, hours, reason, and who covers.
          Sudden Issue (Power Cut, Urgent Travel, Illness)

          Call a colleague immediately, then call Vishnu if no one is free in 15 minutes. The shift stays yours until the override is live in SolarWinds.
